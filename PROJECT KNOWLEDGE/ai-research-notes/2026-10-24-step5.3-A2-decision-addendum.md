## METHOD: the rules every row was decided by

USER RULING 35 binds the whole note: the grader judges the AI by the GAME, never by the AI's own records, except an
HONESTY check of a claim. The spec and `04` queue item 3 add four rules:
- a yield RATE must not be the only curiosity check;
- spacing has no game form;
- a game-read check that no control fails is not registered;
- a control that does not fail a proposed bound is REPORTED.

The rules below were written AFTER the table was seen, which cannot be avoided here. That is why stage B grades once on
held-out seeds.

- **R-ABS (a bound on each run).** It needs three things:
  - every real run is on the passing side;
  - the named control is on the failing side in at least one run of each control arm (the project's existing "the control CAN fail" shape);
  - the candidate bound sits strictly between the two, and both margins are stated.
- **R-PAIR (paired by seed, or a pooled ratio).** It needs two things:
  - the real code beats the control in **at least 10 of 12 paired seeds** (at least 15 of the 18 between-seed pairs);
  - the pooled margin is **at least 5 % of the real value**.
  - WHY 10 of 12 (MEASURED by binomial arithmetic, scratch only): suppose a real code wins each seed with chance p, and stage B grades "wins in at least 60 % of 20 held-out seeds".
    - At p = 10/12 it falsely FAILS 0.3 % of the time.
    - At p = 9/12 it falsely fails 4.1 %, and under a 70 % rule 21 %.
    - A check that fails the healthy code one time in five teaches the reader to bypass it.
  - **The same arithmetic sets every per-personality share rule here** (about 6-7 held-out seeds a personality): "won in >= 60 %" means at least 4 of 6, or 5 of 7. Its false-FAIL chance, binomial:

    | true p | n = 6 (>= 4) | n = 7 (>= 5) | for contrast: "all n", n = 7 |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | 0.80 | 9.9 % | 14.8 % | 79 % |
    | 0.85 | 4.7 % | 7.4 % | 68 % |
    | 0.90 | 1.6 % | 2.6 % | 52 % |
    | 0.95 | 0.2 % | 0.4 % | 30 % |

    The plausible p comes from the data: where every training arm beat NOTRAIN in every pair of a personality (31 of 31 Catcher pairs, 33 of 33 Trainer pairs, of which 29 distinct because PATIENCE's four runs repeat CH1's trajectory), the rule-of-three 95 % lower bound on p is about 0.90. So ">= 60 %" falsely fails at most about 2.6 % (OPEN, STATUS (a): the pairs are dependent; at the unpaired Catcher rate it falsely fails 8-13 %). "All n" (47-52 % at p = 0.9) does not meet the ~5 % target, nor does ">= 70 %" at n = 6 (5 of 6, 11.4 %).
- **Paired controls fail BY CONSTRUCTION** (W7). Where the control is the denominator of the ratio (ERRANDS_OFF in row 8, CH2_OFF in row 11, NOTRAIN in the Trainer and Catcher halves of rows 1-2), its ratio to itself is exactly 1.0 (OPEN, STATUS (d): the pseudo-null below omits LOAF/CH1, which pools to 1.067). Its FAIL is therefore not evidence that the check separates anything, and the bound (1.05, 1.1, 1.3) has no null spread. The only evidence is the real code's own spread (its per-seed wins and pooled margin). The nearest thing to a null, MEASURED here, is the same quantity between two arms that differ in a knob unrelated to errands: on the battle-free frames to the POKéDEX, DEADREST/CH1 pools to **0.977** (per seed 0.889-1.066, SD 0.052) and STALL1/CH1 to **1.028** (0.949-1.143). So codes that are NOT beelines moved the pooled ratio by -2.3 % to +2.8 %. A bound of 1.05 sits 2.2 points above the larger of those. CH2_NOBATTLE has no such pseudo-null: only CH2 and CH2_OFF reach WALLY in this sweep. What this means for stage B is in its rules below.
- **HONESTY**: the row is the AI's own claim, compared with a record the AI cannot write.
  - That record is the game, or an instrument of the harness.
  - The second case is an AMENDMENT to the spec's A1 rule (row 16).
- **DIAGNOSTIC**: no game form exists; or the runner has no game read of it while the sweep grades the same property.
- **REPORTED**: a game or harness form exists, and no control separates it by R-ABS or R-PAIR. No third rule is used: a binary harness record with known answers outside the sweep (L5.PURITY_ENDED) is not given one (row 28).
- **Confidence words.** HIGH means wide margins on every personality. MEDIUM means it separates, but the margin is thin somewhere or the row rests on few runs. LOW means a judgement call that the critic should attack.
- ⚑ marks a row whose separation rests on 1-2 seeds or on one personality.
