## 2. ⛔⛔ THERE IS EXACTLY **ONE** TILE THE BOT CAN FALL THROUGH: `(8,6)`

The fall candidates are the cracked-ice tiles orthogonally adjacent to the island. There is one:
**(8,6)** — section 3's exit tile, sitting directly north of the now-open stairs at (8,5).

‼ **I ALMOST GOT THIS WRONG.** My first pass ranked all 64 ice tiles by B1F route quality and

## 3. ⛔ FROM (8,6) THERE IS **NO TRAINER-FREE ROUTE**. THE EXIT COSTS FIVE BATTLES.

Shortest walk (8,6) → the ladder (11,22) is **20 steps** and crosses the sight lines of
**Andrea, Annika, Bethany, Connie, Daphne**. A route avoiding every sight line **does not exist**
from this fall point. *(By contrast, had the bot been able to fall at (8,19), a 7-step trainer-free
route existed — which is exactly why the unreachable "best" was so tempting.)*

**✅ THE SIGHT-LINE RULE IS DECOMP-VERIFIED, NOT ASSUMED** (`src/trainer_see.c`):
`GetTrainerApproachDistanceSouth` is `trainer.x == player.x && y > trainer.y && y <= trainer.y +
range` — i.e. a trainer sees exactly `range` tiles along its facing — and the other three directions
mirror it. `CheckPathBetweenTrainerAndPlayer` then walks the intervening tiles and returns 0 if any
has a blocking collision, so **walls block sight**. My computation implements exactly this.
