## RULING 19 — teach an OWNED TM when a gym keeps winning

**Where it can help (decomp):** Winona is the best case of all eight gyms — every one of her five mons has an owned
super-effective TM (TM34 Shock Wave 2× Swablu/Skarmory, 4× Pelipper; TM50 Overheat 2× Tropius/Skarmory; TM39 Rock Tomb 2×
on four of five). Juan and Tate & Liza are partial; Roxanne, Brawly, Wattson and Norman have no owned super-effective TM.
Each gym TM is SINGLE-USE (`party_menu.c`) ⇒ a whole-run view (TM34 is wanted at Winona and Juan).
**Expected value (adversarial):** ~1 of the 4 Winona deaths (P1 r3 ~50%, P3 r2 ~30%, P4 r3 ~15%, P3 r1 ~10%). Sceptile,
Swampert, Swellow, Mightyena and most Treecko/Mudkip cores learn neither TM.

**Design (from the three angles):**
* Hook: `_prepare_for_fight`, after maintenance and before the sufficiency gate, when `losses >= 2`; a per-run, per-(boss,
  TM) latch written before work; re-entry checks the world (TM owned, move not known).
* Planner (pure, gated offline): owned TMs from `get_item_bag().tms_hms` (`TM*`, quantity > 0; `can_learn_tm_hm` takes the
  ITEM, a string reads False); boss rosters from `trainer_parties.h` as data (not `ace_types`, which drives three other
  gates); a TM qualifies only if it is ≥2× on the foe that actually ends our fights (live `[TURN]` data), not type-only
  (Shock Wave is 0× on Numel/Camerupt/Whiscash; Levitate).
* Recipient: highest live damage vs that wall via `calculate_move_damage_range`; EXCLUDE HM carriers, `BENCH_SPECIES`,
  hunted counters, anything > 8 levels below the lead; key by PID. **Prefer Shock Wave** (no drop, never misses — Skarmory
  has Sand-Attack). **Never Overheat on a Torchic line before Blaze Kick** (the learn policy would then DECLINE Blaze Kick —
  the move the Torchic winners used).
* Slot: status moves first, never an HM (the game refuses), never the mon's only STAB of a type, keep ≥1 usable attack.
* Execute with `pokemon_league.teach_move_from_bag` ONLY (verified cursor; never `teach_hm_move` / `teach_hm_or_tm` —
  KI-88/KI-89); normalize first and after (its empty-slot path returns with the bag open, undriven).
* Acceptance = the taught move was USED against the wall (rule 7), not "learned".
**Hazards recorded:** Overheat's −2 SpA gets spent on the first foe it tops (the greedy selector); the teach will also fire
in runs that win anyway; the taught mon may never face the wall (defensive lead, no proactive switching — the lead policy is
ruled, do not change it here).

## RULING 20 — organic catching (the fence) with the PC as the runway

**What the limit is:** `decide_dex_catch` — before gym 1 a team target of 4 (`_catch_team_target`, plus the Route-116
`catch_starter_team` spin); after gym 1 a dex catch only at 6/6 (to the box). The runway refusals are ~69% of fence verdicts.
**The history (measured):** raising the pre-gym-1 target 4→6 (`#24-partycap`, 08-05) dropped finish 52.0% → 15.8%, all on
the Balance→Feather (Surf) rung, z=3.06 — cause: "no party mon can learn Surf and the party is full" (127 lines vs 0) with
NO deposit path then. ⚠ Post-badge catches at party 4/5 were NEVER measured (the 09-10 fence extended the result by
reasoning). Since then: make-room deposit (driven 08-13; 36 of 41 freed a slot in the 09-15 corpus), the ping-pong fix
(09-07), PC-first HM withdraw (09-25).

**What would make always-catch backfire (ranked):**
1. **Balls.** The reserve guards ENTRY only; a catch battle throws until caught or empty (Absol: 23 balls in 26.6k frames,
   the run later died "no Poké Balls and no money" for Dive). Always-catch needs ~105-130 balls/run vs one 30-ball purchase
   today; restock is Rustboro-only and only at ≤1 ball.
2. **Money** competes with the League basket (RULINGS 16-18).
3. **The runway failure returns:** make-room deposits only "dead weight" (> 8 levels behind or no damaging move); a strong
   level-even team has none ⇒ HM make-room fails ⇒ the `#24-partycap` signature. Strength, Waterfall and Fly have NO box
   check at all.
4. **Every party member is a §Ch23/§Ch24 trainee** (`battler_identities`, `ch24_trainees_below_floor`).
5. Faint replacement / leads rank type before level (13.9% of faint choices sent a mon ≥15 levels behind) — ruled, do not
   change.
6. PC churn: make-room ignores hunted counters; `_hunted_counter_pids` never expires.
7. The Surf centres (Petalburg/Lavaridge/Fallarbor) are the most-blocked heal walks.

**Phased plan (each phase built, driven, gated on the branch; promotion only after the A/B):**
* **Phase 0 — safety + instrumentation (good regardless of the fence):** a dex-only catch strategy that FLEES at the ball
  reserve inside the battle (do not edit `StoryCatchStrategy` — the shiny strategy inherits it); skip by expected throws
  (catch rate) unless shiny; the per-species attempt cap and health check before gym 1 too; per-species fence verdict
  logging (today one global string ⇒ counts are transitions); balls/₽/frames per catch; every deposit/withdraw outcome.
* **Phase 1 — the PC as the runway:** one deposit policy with a protected set (sole HM carriers, hunted counters until
  their badge, the fighting six by PID — RULING 13 already says the six fighters stay L65-75 and everything else lives in
  the PC); an HM-required make-room override; box-first for Strength, Waterfall, Fly and the counter ladder; centre chosen
  by reachability; ball restock at the nearest reachable mart under a money floor (none after badge 7).
* **Phase 2 — open the fence behind a registered flag:** P2 (post-badge catches at 4/5 → party) and P1 (pre-gym-1
  target 6), separable.
* **Phase 3 — the A/B:** concurrent arms (A HEAD / B P2 / C P1+P2), starters balanced; metrics: per-rung badge
  conversion (Surf rung baseline 78%), HM "party full" raises, make-room outcomes, no-balls deaths, leg-11 refusals,
  §Ch23 trainee counts, owned species. Pre-registered rule: an HM rung −20 pts with z ≥ 2 ⇒ revert P1 first. ~50 runs per
  arm per 12h×4 lanes detects a partycap-sized collapse, not a ≤15-pt move — say so up front.
