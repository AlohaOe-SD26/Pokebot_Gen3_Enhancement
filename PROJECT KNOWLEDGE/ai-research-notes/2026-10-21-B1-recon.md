## What B1 becomes (the coordinator's re-scope, 2026-10-21; a spec comes before any build)

1. **The grade reads the GAME:** every graded human-likeness yield, and the frames it is judged on, comes from the
   harness's own recorders, never from the AI's actions, `world.jsonl` or records.
2. **The grader takes no bound from AI code:** `rate_step5` stops importing `personality.ERRANDS` and the other AI
   constants to SET a bound. A bound is written once, harness-side.
3. **The AI stops copying graded numbers:** `YIELD_TILES_MIN`, `YIELD_TILE_RATE` and the formula are re-derived from
   the AI's own purpose (PF-14), or the grade moves to an outcome the AI does not compute.
4. **R13's rule is written down** before any AI-player tuner mode: fitness = progress READ FROM THE GAME plus a held-out
   judge that neither the tuner nor the AI can read.

**Cost:** this changes the step-5 grader, so step 5 is RE-GRADED (`rate_step5`, a graded sweep, hours). Build it with
the next graded round (step 5.3), not as a quick gate. Not 80 + 80 lines.
