### Design gaps I had to decide (please rule)
- **Caps:** the damage cap and the status cap each block only their own branch. The no-progress cap makes the rest of the battle throw-only.
- **"No-progress" is undefined in the design.** My definition: a DAMAGE or STATUS turn after which, at the next decision on a later turn, the target's HP is not lower and it has gained no status.
  - Consequence: two missed actions trigger throw-only before 3 status tries can be spent.
  - So W57h is built from 3 tries that each landed and then woke.
- **`first=1`** marks the first DAMAGE or STATUS verdict; a DEFER does not count.
- **Battle-type DEFER** covers every flag except IS_MASTER (bit 2) and RECORDED_IS_MASTER (bit 31). That is wider than the design's list.
- **Stage-2 divergence rules:**
  - Flash Fire: any lead with a live Flash Fire ability counts as boosted, because I don't read the boost bit.
  - Charge: any Charge on our lead counts, which also covers "Hidden Power under Charge".
  - Badge 7: counts when we hold badge 7 and the move is a special type or Hidden Power.
- **Fault scopes:**
  - MASTERBALL changes only the ball a THROW returns, not the ball used for the odds check or E_gain.
  - NOFLOOR and NOPP apply to stage 1 only.
- **Choosing between admissible moves:** the largest guaranteed damage wins, ties go to the lowest slot.
- **Log format:**
  - lines: `act=<V> t= sp= pid= hp= st= tmv= slot= move= ball= reason= snap=<json>`, and `[WEAKEN-OBS] would=… first=`;
  - spaces inside values become `_`;
  - an out-of-scope kind makes no read and logs nothing;
  - an in-scope DEFER does log a line.
