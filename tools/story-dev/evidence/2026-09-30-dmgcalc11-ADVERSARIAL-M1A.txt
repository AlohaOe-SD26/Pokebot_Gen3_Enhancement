**First-run difference.** On the first run every mutant's set was its expected set plus `SW`, and `SW` was also red on the unmutated code. Its failing line showed the sweep counted 5,368,464 tuples. My hard-coded expected total (5,368,992) was an arithmetic slip; the correct figure is 17·289·2·2·258 + 289·2·2·258 = 5,368,464. So the check was wrong, not the subject. **Nothing in the predictions failed to reproduce.** Four things you should know:
- The port reports a no-effect hit as 0 HP. The decomp's `gBattleMoveDamage` is actually 1 in cases like Ground vs Flying/Rock (a later type row floors the 0 back to 1), but the HP update is skipped. P2 and m12 pin this, and it does not affect any bound.
- The witnesses are printed, not pinned as rows, as you asked. The design wanted them as rows, but they largely coincide with the anchors: the crit "under" witness is D-A1's tuple, the non-crit "over" is D-O1's, and the crit + Charge "under" is D-A2 with the defender pair in the other order.
- The gate is not registered in `run_gates.py`, since I was told to change no other file.
- The worktree holds other agents' uncommitted work (`dmgaudit.py`, `probe_dmgaudit.py`, `audit_damage_calc.py`, edits to `battle_action_selection.py` and `battle_handler.py`, the 29 registry). I didn't touch any of it.
