## 4. A real classifier gap (not a mutant)

`C:sport` (`audit_damage_calc.py:422`) and the pinch DIP (`:488`) compare the move's **listed** type.

The game uses Hidden Power's **rolled** type:
- `battle_script_commands.c:8909-8912` sets `dynamicMoveType`, and `:1295` passes it to the damage calculation.
- `pokemon.c:3124-3127` uses it as the type, and `:3215` (sport) and `:3219` (pinch) test that type.

Our calculator also uses the rolled type (`_util.py:25-27`, `:408-414`). Two consequences:
- **Hidden Power rolled Grass, Overgrow, and a dip to ≤ 1/3 is ACCEPTED.** The game applies 1.5× and ours does not, so the result is a false FAIL.
- **Hidden Power rolled Fire under Water Sport is ACCEPTED.** It is halved, so it pollutes the lower tail.

`NR-hp-rolled-type` (in `proposed.py`) is **RED on the current code**: both cases come out `ACCEPTED`, `reasons=[]`.

The fix is to use `hidden_power_type(me.ivs)` for move 237 in both places, and in `_state_reasons`'s `mtype`.

A related note: SUPERPOWER and OVERHEAT are in Tier A, but their guaranteed self stat-drop always triggers `our-attack-stage-changed`. So they can never be ACCEPTED — they are in the whitelist in name only (this loses samples; it is not a harm).

All status, side and action constants in the audit match pokeemerald `include/constants/battle.h`.

**The classifier:**
- **Exit codes:** 0 PASS, 1 FAIL, 2 INSUFFICIENT, 3 instrument failure (raw line count ≠ parsed count, or the logger's cap marker `event log size cap reached` from `debug_logger.py`).
- **Parsing is strict to v=1.** An unknown field, `v≠1`, a wrong hex width or `fNone` counts as unparsed, so the audit exits 3. If production ever adds a field, it has to bump `v`.
- **Production is compatible.** The production module appeared while I worked; a read-only look at its format strings matches my parser field for field: `bide` is decoded signed, and `eff` is the plain moves.json string.
- **The whitelist** is a constant in the classifier, keyed on moves.json `effect`, with Surf (57) and Struggle (165) keyed on move index.
- **ACCEPT_MARGIN = 3** (a proposal for the user). The M1a bounds are the pre-registered 4, and 8 with Charge.
- **Outputs:**
  - the raw and parsed counts;
  - per-reason counts;
  - accepted samples split wild/trainer, by tier and by kind;
  - per effect and per stratum: max(obs − crit_max), max(obs/crit_max), the counts over crit_max + margin and over crit_max + the M1a bound, and hits below the non-crit min;
  - the crit-ambiguity list;

  - one line per BEFORE;

**Where F4 was ambiguous, and what I chose (each choice can only exclude more, never accept more):**
1. **Trainer KO at the AFTER read.** The foe slot then holds the replacement, so I don't apply the foe's per-mon checks to it; our-side and side-level checks still run. What that could miss is covered by a stratum I added, `foe-after-unobservable`: it fires when our move is Tier B, or when the foe's chosen move could have changed what our hit met (its Def/SpD stages, its types, going underground/underwater/airborne, minimizing, a sport).
2. **I added `foe-profile-changed`** (the foe's types, ability, Def or SpD stat differ between reads, e.g. Conversion or Transform), by analogy with F4's "Def/SpD stage changed". It is not in F4.
3. **Action-based reasons and Pursuit are read at BEFORE only.** The entry read resets actions to 255 and moves to 0 (visible in the Step-0 reads), and a battle-end read just repeats BEFORE's. False Swipe's condition is defined on "HP before", so it has no AFTER half either.
4. **One over-bound hit is FAIL at any sample size.** F4 doesn't say what wins when a run is both under 300 and over the bound; I followed B1's "one is enough to fail it for good".
5. **Wild vs trainer is a label, not an exclusion**, because the predictions table accepts wild samples.
6. **Doubles are checked after the sample checks.** A doubles turn where our move didn't run is "not a sample"; one where it ran is in the doubles stratum.
7. **The self-check runs on every validly paired singles turn with the same foe and no KO, whatever our action was.** I added three exclusions beyond F4's enumerated list: the foe's own heal moves, Pain Split, and a foe Substitute.
8. **One consequence of choice 1:** an Intimidate replacement on a trainer KO would flag "our Atk stage changed" and exclude a good sample.
