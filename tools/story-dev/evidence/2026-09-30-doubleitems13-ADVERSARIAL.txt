## Should-fix

**SF-1: in doubles, the D10 filter turns "no status cures" into "cure only with a Full Restore". Confirmed in memory.**
- **Sequence:** flag `1`, in-scope double. Our active Pokémon is asleep or frozen, and the HP policy declines (above the fraction, losing race, or no HP item). The bag holds Full Heal ×1 and Full Restore ×1.
- **What happens:** `story_mode.py:1186-1189` hands `hp_only(medicine)` to `choose_status_or_revive_item`. That list still contains Full Restore (parameter 255, kept by `double_items.py:129-133`). Rule 1 then walks `(*specific, "Full Heal", "Lava Cookie", "Full Restore")` (`battle_items.py:256`) and returns the Full Restore.
- **Measured** with the gate's own fakes (`scratchpad/d13hunt/cure_probe.py`), Sceptile asleep at 100/100 and at 90/100:
  - double, side 0 → `UseItem(Full Restore, 1)`;
  - singles, same Pokémon → `UseItem(Full Heal, 1)`;
  - bag with Full Heal only → double gives the parent's attack.
- **Why it matters:** D10 says "a status cure … is not requested in a double", and battle_items rule 3 says "the scarce Full Restore last". Both are inverted: the cheaper cure is filtered out and the scarcest item is spent on sleep. This is reachable at Tate & Liza (Hypnosis) whenever a Full Restore is held, and in the rematch era.
- **Safety is unaffected:** parameter > 0 sends it to the bag-count verdict (`story_mode.py:1215-1219`), so a refusal is still counted.
- **Gate gap:** DQ07 (`probe_double_items.py:439-444`) only tests a Full-Heal-only bag.
- **Fix:** in doubles, skip the cure policy entirely (`if doubles: item = None` instead of calling it), mirror that in observe (`story_mode.py:6591`), and add a row: asleep at 100/100 with Full Heal + Full Restore → the parent's action.

**SF-2: the bag-open budget (3 tries × 30 frames, `battle_action_selection.py:1220-1233`) has 16 frames of margin for battler 0's first item request of a turn in a double. Latent, not observed failing.**
- **Measured live** in `evidence/2026-09-30-doubleitems13-ARMS.txt`:

- **Failure path:** about 17 more frames of delay and the executor returns ("bag-did-not-open … the strategy will re-decide") while the bag is still opening.
