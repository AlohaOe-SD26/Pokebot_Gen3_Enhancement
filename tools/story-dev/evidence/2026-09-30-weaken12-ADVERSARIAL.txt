### Production defects (the real code gives a verdict that is unsafe or breaks D4's premise that the target "can hurt itself in NO way")

**PD1 (a real knockout path, reachable in Emerald): Trace on a party member who is not the lead.**
- The retaliation clause reads each non-lead's ability as stored in the party. A Trace mon that comes in after a faint copies the target's live ability:
  - Trace is set on switch-in (`battle_util.c:2561-2566`) and copied from the opposing battler (`:3003-3043`).
  - Rough Skin then does maxHP/16 per contact (`:2753-2766`), on every throw turn — the "repeats every turn" hazard D4 rejected numeric floors over.
- Snapshot: S0 + `party=("Torrent","Trace")` + target live ability Rough Skin, types Water/Dark, moves Leer/Bite/Rage/Focus Energy. The real verdict is **DAMAGE(Seismic Toss)**. The control with `party=("Torrent","Rough Skin")` gives THROW (retaliation).
- Reach:
  - Our side: Ralts, Kirlia or Gardevoir with Trace (Ralts is wild on Route 102).
  - Targets: Carvanha (Rough Skin), Qwilfish (Poison Point), Shroomish (Effect Spore) with contact moves. Synchronize targets work the same way.
  - It needs the lead to faint after the hit.
- Suggested fix, in `_self_harm_reason`: if any party subject is "Trace", add `target.ability` to the subjects. A Trace lead is already covered by its live ability.

**PD2 (latent; no wild Pokémon in Emerald has it): Pain Split is missing from the no-self-harm list.**
- If the target moves first, Pain Split sets its own HP to (ours + its)/2 before our hit lands (`battle_script_commands.c:8049-8066`; `battle_scripts_1.s:1239-1252`). X was bounded against the HP at the start of the turn.
- Snapshot: S0 + target move slot 2 = Pain Split + our hp = 5. Verdict: DAMAGE, X = 13. After Pain Split the target is at (22+5)/2 = 13, so 13 − 13 = 0 and it faints.
- Only Misdreavus learns it by level (L37, per `species.json`), and it is not wild in Emerald.
- Step 11's classifier already counts PAIN_SPLIT as the foe hurting itself.
- Fix: add `"PAIN_SPLIT"` to `SELF_HARM_EFFECTS`.

**PD3 (latent; no reach): confusion berries.**
- Figy, Wiki, Mago, Aguav and Iapapa (`confuse_*` hold effects) trigger at hp ≤ max/2, heal 1/8, and confuse the holder on a disliked flavour (`battle_util.c:3213-3228`). The weaken hit is what takes the target to ≤ 50%, and the confusion self-hit can then knock it out.
- `held_items_rse` has no wild Pokémon holding one, so reach is zero.
- `target.item` is in the snapshot but no damage rule uses it.

**PD4 (no reach): a party member with Sand Stream** (Tyranitar, trade-only) would start a sandstorm when it comes in after a faint. The weather rule only looks at the weather in effect and at the target's moves.

**PD5 (very low): the Struggle horizon H does not count some turns where no ball is thrown.**
- Uncounted: failed flee attempts after the balls run out (possible against any faster wild Pokémon), and a stage-2 RECHARGE turn (Hyper Beam is in Tier A).
- The result would be Struggle recoil of at least 1 on a 1-HP target. The +4 slack, usually 3 unused status tries, normally covers it.

**Checked and NOT a defect:**
- **Dream Eater + Liquid Ooze.** Emerald's Dream Eater script (`battle_scripts_1.s:425-461`) has no Liquid Ooze branch. Only the Absorb family (`:345`) and Leech Seed (`:3271`) check it, so `DRAIN_EFFECTS={ABSORB,LEECH_SEED}` is exactly right.

### The builder's own design-gap decisions (safety verdict for each)
1. **No-progress cap (two acting turns with no progress → throw-only). Safe.** It only ever adds THROW-ONLY, and a ball can't knock anything out. It is conservative: a hit that landed but was healed back counts as no progress.
2. **Damage and status caps each block only their own branch. Safe.** Status can't knock anything out. A later damage turn (for example after a faint brings in a new lead) is still held by the floor and the 50% rule, and the damage cap is still 1 per battle.
3. **Battle-type DEFER on every flag except IS_MASTER and RECORDED_IS_MASTER. Safe and conservative.** A DEFER is the parent's baseline turn. The gate pinned only 5 bits; P-btsweep pins all 32.
4. **Stage 2's divergence rules. Conservative, since they are broader than step 11's list**: Charge on any move, badge 7 also covering Hidden Power, Marvel Scale with either side statused. But only Guts-with-a-burn was gated; the other five were unpinned (V98–V105).
5. **The MASTERBALL, NOFLOOR and NOPP fault scopes. They match their names:** NOFLOOR and NOPP apply to stage 1 only. All faults are unreachable with the flag unset, because `decide` DEFERs first and the adapter returns before building the snapshot. NOFLOOR is a knockout switch by design. It is read from the environment on every call, which is the project's usual pattern.
6. **Move choice (largest guaranteed damage; ties go to the lowest slot). Safe.** Every admissible candidate already passed the floor or the stage-2 bound. But it was unpinned (V73, V74, V97); P-max, P-tie and P-s2max pin it.
7. **An in-scope DEFER writes a log line. Harmless.** It is telemetry only: the flag is set, the action returned is None, and D6's "no line with the flag unset" still holds.
