  ⛔⛔ 9 RUNG(S) STILL LEAK: L12, L18-L20, L24, L35-L38

     ⇒ AT THOSE LEVELS THE RULING'S MECHANISM DOES NOT HOLD. The selector minimises
       `abs(hi - team_level)`, which finds the CLOSEST band — not necessarily one the
       trainee out-levels. Where the closest band's top is 2+ above the trainee, the
       under-levelled trigger still fires and the finisher still banks an equal share.

     ⭐ WHAT A LEG MUST THEREFORE DO, and this is the actionable finding:
       do NOT simply ask for the closest band. Either
         (a) require `trainee >= hi - 1` and accept a lower band when none qualifies, or
         (b) accept the leak at those rungs and CHECK the ceiling afterwards.
       ⛔ (b) is the shape that produced L89 finishers before the ruling. Prefer (a).
