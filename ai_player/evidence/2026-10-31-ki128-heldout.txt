Between two consecutive crossings c and d of one edge in one intent there is NEWS when any of: (N1) a line '[AI-WORLD] novelty at f<M>:' lies between them in the log (the AI's told novelty: a story flag, a new Pokemon, more items, experience, new words); (N2) a line 'MILESTONE <id> done' lies between them; (N3) the world memory's tiles_learned in the first world.jsonl line holding d's transition (its frame, both maps, how 'connection') is greater than in the first line holding c's (a new tile seen); (N4) the number of maps with visits in those two lines rose (a new map). A crossing with no world line gets no N3/N4 news (strict) and is counted as unmatched in the check's text. An ALTERNATION is a maximal sequence of consecutive crossings of one edge in one intent with no news between any two neighbours; its LENGTH is its number of crossings. Bound: every alternation's length <= 5. (Crossings with news between them are no ping-pong, B-C1: an intent may cross one edge more than 5 times in all.)
K8.WORLD_SIZE: every run's world.jsonl, measured before its deletion, is under 300000000 bytes (300 MB).
K8.FROZEN: the frozen-tree watch (provenance.FrozenTree) saw no change of the code under test from the batch's start to its verdict.
K8.LEFTOVER: no python process whose command line names ai-ki128-ho- is left after the batch.
REPORTED (information, never counted): K8.RUSTBORO: RUSTBORO_CITY visited by the game's truth (VISITED_RUSTBORO_CITY = 1 at the end) in at least 4 of 6 (ROUTE104 north has trainers; other blockers are A1's to measure). K8.ENDED: who ended each run (the harness's own stop record). Per run: map changes of both kinds, edge crossings, the longest alternation, the woods exit, the AI's outcome claim beside the game's flag.
K8.EDGE_WINDOW (information, never counted): in every run, at most 10 crossings of one edge within any 3000 frames, INTENTS IGNORED (round 2's D2w criterion, 2026-10-31-KI129B-BUILD-REPORT.md; added before registration because the per-intent K8.ALTERNATION cannot see a ping-pong carried across intents). Read from ai_player.log: every CROSSING line as K8.ALTERNATION defines it, its EDGE the unordered pair of maps and its frame N from '(<actor>, f<N>)'; per edge, the most crossings whose frames lie within 3000 of each other (the last minus the first <= 3000). Bound: that number <= 10 for every edge. A run with any crossing line without a frame is not read by this check (strict). Also reported per run (information): the longest no-news alternation with intents ignored (K8.ALTERNATION's rule over one pooled intent).
VERDICT: PASS iff every GRADED check PASSES. Nothing is tuned to a grader (B1): a miss is reported, never re-run on new seeds.
